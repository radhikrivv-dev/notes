Frequently Repeated Physics Questions

⚡ Electric Potential & Potential Difference (Group A and B)

  1. Definition of Potential and Potential Difference
    Define electric potential or potential difference (V or p.d.) between two points in an electric field.
  2. Derivation for a Point Charge
    Derive the mathematical expression for electric potential at a point due to an isolated point charge (V = 1/4πε₀ × Q/r).
  3. Potential Difference between Two Points
    Derive the expression for the potential difference between two specific points (often given as distances r₁ and r₂) within an electric field.
  4. Equipotential Surfaces
    Define equipotential surfaces and derive the expression for electric potential due to a charge.

💡 Theory / Derivation Questions (Group A & B)

  1. Wheatstone Bridge Balance Condition
    Derive the balancing condition of a Wheatstone bridge circuit using Kirchhoff's laws.
  2. Bohr's Atomic Model Energy Equation
    State Bohr's postulates and derive the expression for the total energy of an electron in the nth orbit of a hydrogen atom.
  3. Velocity of Sound & Laplace Correction
    Write Newton's formula for the velocity of sound in air and explain how Laplace corrected it.
  4. Young's Double Slit Experiment
    Describe Young's double-slit experiment for light interference and show that the fringe widths for dark and bright fringes are equal.
  5. Biot-Savart's Law Applications
    State Biot-Savart's law and use it to find the magnetic field intensity due to a straight current-carrying conductor or at the center/axis of a circular coil.
  6. State and Prove Brewster's Law
    Formulate polarization of light and mathematically prove Brewster's Law.
  7. Energy Stored in a Stretched Wire
    Define stress and strain and derive an expression for the total energy stored or energy density in a stretched elastic wire.
  8. Energy Stored in a Charged Capacitor
    Define capacitance and derive the expression for electrostatic energy stored within a charged capacitor.
  9. Galvanometer Conversions
    Explain the standard experimental methods to convert a galvanometer into an ammeter or a voltmeter.
  10. Semiconductor Diodes as Rectifiers
    Explain the full-wave or half-wave rectification mechanism using P-N junction diodes with relevant circuit diagrams.

🔢 Identical / Highly Repeated Numerical Problems (Group C)

  1. Velocity of Sound at 27°C
    Calculate the velocity of sound in air at 27°C if the density of air at STP is 1.29 kg/m³ and γ = 1.4.
  2. Radioactive Source Decaying to 1/128th
    A radioactive substance has decayed to 1/128th of its initial activity after 50 days. Calculate its half-life.
  3. Temperature to Double Velocity of Sound
    Find the temperature at which the velocity of sound becomes twice that at 27°C (or 127°C).
  4. Equilateral Triangle Resultant Force
    Three point charges are placed at the corners of an equilateral triangle. Find the resultant force on the target charge.
  5. Photoelectric Effect
    Monochromatic light of 6000 Å falls on a metal surface. Calculate the maximum kinetic energy, stopping potential, or velocity of emitted electrons.
  6. Copper Wire Drift Velocity
    Calculate the current density and drift velocity of electrons in a copper wire of diameter 1.02 mm carrying a current of 1.67 A.
  7. Capillary Rise of Liquid
    Calculate the height of capillary rise/depression using the given surface tension, density, radius, and angle of contact.
  8. Helium Nucleus Binding Energy
    Calculate the binding energy or binding energy per nucleon of a helium nucleus using the given masses.
